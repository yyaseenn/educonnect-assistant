Hi, what do you need help with?

Ask me anything general and I'll answer from EduConnect's approved guides, with the source every time. I can't see your grades, fees or personal records, so for anything personal or sensitive I'll connect you with staff. Please don't type personal details.

How this answer was produced

Our technical workflow, running live for every question.

  1. 1
    Scope & privacy check
    Is this a general question we're allowed to answer?
  2. 2
    Search approved guides
    Ranked search over the sample guides only
  3. 3
    Draft answer
    AI writes from the matched passages only
  4. 4
    Check against source
    Does the answer cite and quote a real passage?
  5. 5
    Answer or hand off
    Reply with source, or route to staff
